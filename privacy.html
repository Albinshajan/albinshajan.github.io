Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

kids-shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the ALBIN SHAJAN YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, kids-shorts asks for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to manage their captions (the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl permissions). It uses these only to upload the owner's videos, set each video's title, description, tags and privacy settings, and add caption files.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. kids-shorts has no server and no database, and it does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, including anything about people who watch the videos.

YouTube API Services

kids-shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page. Deleting the token file from the owner's computer also removes kids-shorts' access.

Children

The videos are made for children and are marked Made for kids on YouTube. kids-shorts does not collect any information from children or from any viewer.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
